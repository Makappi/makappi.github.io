Set up Biscuits Bundles

  1. FAQ
  2. Integrations
  3. Set up Biscuits Bundles

Let customers compose a pack in your native app, from a Home banner, a product link or a dedicated menu entry.

1. Prepare your pack in Biscuits Bundles

Install Biscuits Bundles in Shopify and create your pack there. This integration supports one selection step, variable quantities with an increment of one, and a percentage discount, either fixed or based on quantity tiers. V1 supports one variant at a time per product and up to 249 variants in total. Mandatory selections, custom fields, fixed-price bundles and multiple steps are not supported yet.

The product page must contain the Biscuits configurator and be accessible on your online store. The online store must be publicly accessible, without a storefront password. Product choices and bundle rules remain managed in Biscuits.

2. Publish the products on the Makappi sales channel

  1. Open Shopify → Products and select the parent bundle product created by Biscuits.
  2. Set its status to Active.
  3. In Publishing / Sales channels and apps, click Manage and select Makappi.
  4. Click Done, then Save on the product page. Selecting the channel without saving the product is not enough.
  5. Repeat for all component products and check their availability in the markets where your app sells.

3. Configure the Makappi integration

In Settings → Integrations → Biscuits Bundles, enable the integration, then save and publish the Makappi configuration. No packs, product URLs or version limits need to be entered. When a customer opens a product, the app automatically reads its online product page and opens the native configurator if it contains a supported Biscuits bundle. Regular products keep their standard product page.

4. Add an entry point

  • Collections: enable “Show build your pack below collections” in the integration settings. Customize the label in each language and choose an optional image. The button appears below the main collections list, using the menu style.
  • Menu: add Biscuits Bundles from the bottom of the block library, then customize its label, icon and position. No product selection is required.
  • Home: in a banner or slideshow image, choose Select a Destination → Build your pack. No URL is needed.

These three destinations discover products tagged biscuits-bundle and published on the Makappi channel. A single pack opens its product selection directly. If several exist, the customer first chooses which pack to build. Keep this tag on parent products. Links to a specific product still open that product’s configurator.

Disabling the integration hides its dedicated menu entries and stops automatic detection. Manage bundle availability in Shopify and Biscuits, and remove links to discontinued products.

5. Test before sharing with customers

Choose variants and quantities, check availability and wait for Shopify to calculate the price before adding the pack. The initial estimate is replaced by the bundle price before the add button becomes available. Shopify calculates the final price in the cart; confirm it and the checkout total in each selling currency, including applicable discounts. Test quantity changes, removal of the last pack, two different selections of the same pack and a cart containing regular products. Open checkout with a customer account if your app requires one.

Troubleshooting

If loading fails, check that the public product page contains the Biscuits configurator, that its rules are supported, and that the parent and components are published on Makappi. A failed detection blocks the product screen and offers a retry instead of allowing an unconfigured bundle to be added. After a change in Biscuits, allow up to one minute for the configuration cache to refresh, then reload the app screen. A market restriction or an out-of-stock variant may prevent selection even when the product is published.

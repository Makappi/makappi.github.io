How does Makappi Cart Hold work?

  1. FAQ
  2. Integrations
  3. How does Makappi Cart Hold work?

Makappi Cart Hold reserves the stock of a product when a customer adds it to the cart in your mobile app. The customer sees a countdown on the cart line (for example "Reserved — 59:32"). If they don't complete their purchase before the end of the countdown, the reservation is released and the stock becomes available again for other customers.

This feature is designed for high-demand sales, especially live shopping sessions with Live Me Up, where many customers buy the same limited-stock products at the same time.

Availability

Cart Hold is currently in restricted access and is enabled shop by shop by the Makappi team. Contact us if you would like to try it. Activating it may require re-authorizing the Makappi app on Shopify, because stock reservation needs additional inventory permissions.

Configuration

  • Once enabled, the integration appears in your backoffice under "Settings" > "Integrations" > "Enabled integrations"
  • Use the "Reservations active" switch to turn reservations on or off without losing your settings — for example right before and after a live shopping session. Turning it off releases all active reservations and restores the stock
  • Set the reservation duration in minutes (default: 60 minutes), then publish your app configuration

How it works

  • When a customer adds a product to the cart, the available stock is decreased on Shopify
  • When the customer opens the checkout, the reservation is released so the payment can go through
  • While the customer is paying (about 10 minutes of grace period), the released stock is protected inside the app: other app users cannot reserve it
  • If the customer removes the product or lets the countdown expire, the stock is restored automatically

Good to know

  • Your available stock will move automatically in Shopify as customers add and remove products — this is expected
  • Only products with tracked inventory and the "stop selling when out of stock" policy are reserved
  • Stock is reserved at your shop's primary location
  • If there is not enough stock to reserve, the product is still added to the cart normally, without a countdown
  • While the product is in the cart, the reservation protects your customer against everyone: the stock is actually decreased on Shopify, so nobody can buy it, whether from the app, the online store or any other sales channel. The only exposed window is between the moment the customer taps checkout and the moment they complete the payment, because the stock has to be released so Shopify accepts the payment. During that short window, other app users still cannot reserve it (grace period), but a buyer from another sales channel (online store, POS…) could take it. The ideal setup is to use Cart Hold on products sold only through the mobile app sales channel (typical for live shopping exclusives) — otherwise you should accept this small window

FAQ

Why is my stock changing on its own?

This is Cart Hold reserving and releasing stock as customers add products to their cart in the mobile app.

A customer says the countdown disappeared, is that a bug?

When the customer opens the checkout, the reservation is released (so Shopify accepts the payment). If they come back to the cart without buying, the products are reserved again. An expired countdown shows a "Reservation expired" badge instead.

Does the reservation block the customer's own purchase?

No. The reservation is released the moment the customer opens the checkout.

Can another customer "steal" the product?

Not while it is in the cart: the stock is decreased on Shopify, so nobody can buy it from any sales channel during the countdown. The only exposed window is between the moment the customer taps checkout and the moment the payment is completed, because the stock has to be released for Shopify to accept the payment. During that window, other app users still cannot reserve it (grace period), but a buyer from another sales channel (online store, POS…) could take it. This is why we recommend using Cart Hold on products sold only through the mobile app channel.

How do I pause reservations between two live sessions?

Use the "Reservations active" switch in the integration settings and publish. Active reservations are released immediately and the stock is restored; the duration setting is kept for next time.

What happens if the app crashes or the customer closes it?

Nothing is lost: the reservation simply expires at the end of the configured duration and the stock is restored automatically.

What if I uninstall the Makappi app while reservations are active?

Shopify revokes Makappi's access when the app is uninstalled, so active reservations cannot be restored automatically. Check your stock levels after uninstalling.

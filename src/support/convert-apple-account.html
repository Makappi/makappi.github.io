Convert an individual Apple Developer account to an organization

  1. FAQ
  2. Apple Developer account

Makappi is not compatible with Individual Apple Developer accounts. To publish your app with Makappi, you must have an Organization account. If your account is currently registered as an Individual, you need to ask Apple to convert it by following the steps below.

Before you start

Have the following ready:

  • Account Holder access. The request must be made by the company's founder or cofounder.
  • A company recognized as a legal entity. A trade name or sole proprietorship alone does not qualify for an organization membership.
  • Your company's D-U-N-S Number. You can look it up or request one for free through Apple's D-U-N-S information page.
  • Your company's legal name, address, and contact details, matching its D-U-N-S record, plus business registration documents if Apple requests them.

Submit your request to Apple

  1. Sign in to your Apple Developer account using the Account Holder's Apple Account.
  2. Open Membership and check that your account type is Individual. If it already shows Organization, no conversion is needed.
  3. Click Need to edit this information?, then choose the link to provide your organization's new details (Provide your updated information or Provide your new organization information, depending on the screen).
  4. Complete the Individual to Organization Membership Update form with your company's details and D-U-N-S Number.
  5. Check the information and submit your request to Apple.

Can't find the option? Open Apple's official conversion request form while signed in as the Account Holder.

Follow up on the conversion

Apple reviews your request and may contact you to verify your company or ask for supporting documents. Check the email address associated with your account and respond to Apple's requests. Processing time depends on the verification required.

For more information, see Apple's official account update guidance.

Identify app customers: customer metafield and segments

  1. FAQ
  2. Customer metafield and segments

Identify customers signed in to your app using the Makappi App User customer metafield, and find those who ordered through the app with Shopify segments.

  • Use the Makappi App User customer metafield
  • Find customers who ordered through the app

Customers signed in to the app: Makappi App User

Makappi automatically updates the Makappi App User customer metafield to identify customers signed in to their account in the iOS or Android app, even if they have not placed an order.

View the field on a customer profile

In Shopify, go to Customers, open a customer profile, then find Makappi App User under Metafields.

  • True: the customer signed in to their account in the app. The field is also updated when they open the app with an existing session.
  • False: the field is reset to false when the customer explicitly signs out of the app, if the update can be sent.

Filter your reports in Shopify Analytics

The Makappi App User metafield is also available under Filters in all Shopify Analytics views. Add this filter and select Yes to analyze data for identified app customers, or No for customers whose field is false.

Create a segment using this field

  1. Go to Customers → Segments → Create segment.
  2. In the segment filters, find the Makappi App User metafield and select true.
  3. Save the segment with a name such as Customers signed in to the app. You can then export it as described below.

This segment helps you find identified app customers, including those who have not placed an order yet.

This field is an approximate indicator: it does not cover guests and cannot be used to count installations. Uninstalling the app or using multiple devices can leave a value that no longer reflects the current situation. Customers who are already signed in are included progressively when they next open a compatible app version.

Customers who ordered through the app

Create a Shopify customer segment to find customers who have placed at least one order through the Makappi sales channel. You can then export the customer list if needed.

1. Open customer segments

In your Shopify admin, go to Customers → Segments, then click Create segment.

2. Describe the customers you want to find

Copy the sentence below and paste it into the segment description field. Then click the arrow on the right.

Customers who placed orders created by the app 'Makappi, Mobile App Builder'.

3. Save the generated segment

Shopify generates the query and displays the matching customers. Check that the description refers to customers who placed orders through Makappi, then click Save.

The segment can be empty if no customers match yet. It updates automatically as customers place orders through Makappi.

4. Name your segment

Enter a name, such as Ordered from App, then click Save.

5. Export the customer list (optional)

Once the segment is saved, open More actions → Export to export the customer list.

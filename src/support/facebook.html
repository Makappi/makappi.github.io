The Facebook integration requires a Facebook Application ID. You can create a Facebook app from Meta for Developers and copy the Application ID into Makappi.

How to get your Facebook Application ID

  1. Go to developers.facebook.com and log in with your Facebook account.
  2. Open My Apps. If you already have a Facebook app for your store, select it and go directly to step 5.
  3. If you do not have an app yet, click Create App, choose the Business app type, then continue.
  4. Enter your app display name and contact email. Select a Business Account if needed, then create the app.
  5. Copy the App ID displayed at the top-left of the Facebook app dashboard, then paste it into the Facebook integration in Makappi.

Facebook

  1. FAQ
  2. Facebook

The Facebook integration requires the Application ID and the Client Token of your Meta app. You can create a Facebook app from Meta for Developers and copy the Application ID into Makappi.

How to get your Facebook Application ID

  1. Go to developers.facebook.com and log in with your Facebook account.
  2. Open My Apps. If you already have a Facebook app for your store, select it and go directly to step 5.
  3. If you do not have an app yet, click Create App, choose the Business app type, then continue.
  4. Enter your app display name and contact email. Select a Business Account if needed, then create the app.
  5. Copy the App ID displayed at the top-left of the Facebook app dashboard, then paste it into the Facebook integration in Makappi.
  1. In the left menu, go to App settings > Basic.
  2. Fill in the required basic settings, especially the Privacy Policy URL and Category, then save your changes.

How to get your Client Token

Since recent versions of the Meta SDK, events are only accepted when the app also sends the Client Token of your Meta app.

  1. In your Meta app, go to App settings > Advanced.
  2. In the Security section, copy the Client token.
  3. Paste it into the Client Token field of the Facebook integration in Makappi, next to the Application ID, then save.

Without a Client Token, the app keeps working normally but Meta rejects its events: your campaigns cannot measure installs, add to carts or purchases.

Add the iOS and Android platforms

In your Meta app, go to App settings > Basic, click Add platform at the bottom of the page and add iOS and Android. The Makappi dashboard shows the values of your app, ready to copy, in Settings > Integrations > Facebook.

Get your Android key hashes

Meta identifies your Android app by the certificates that sign it. Google Play signs the app installed by your customers, so you need the key hash of the app signing key, plus the one of the upload key.

  1. In Google Play Console, open your app, then Test and release > App integrity > App signing.
  2. Copy the SHA-1 certificate fingerprint of the App signing key certificate (for example AB:CD:EF:..., 20 pairs of characters). Do not use the SHA-256.
  3. In Makappi, go to Settings > Integrations > Facebook and paste it in the Key Hashes converter. Copy the generated key hash (it ends with =) into the Key Hashes field in Meta.
  4. Repeat with the SHA-1 of the Upload key certificate, shown on the same page, then save your changes in Meta.

Meta expects the SHA-1 encoded in base64, not the fingerprint displayed by Google Play: that is why the conversion is needed. It runs in your browser, nothing is sent.

Events sent to Meta

The app sends Meta standard events, which your campaigns can optimize for: product view (ViewContent), add to cart (AddToCart), add to wishlist (AddToWishlist), checkout start (InitiateCheckout), purchase (Purchase, with amount and currency) and account creation (CompleteRegistration). Products are identified by their Shopify variant ID.

You can check that events are received in Meta Events Manager > Test events.

How to integrate Flits Wishlist?

  1. FAQ
  2. Integrations
  3. How to integrate Flits Wishlist?

Add Flits to your Mobile App to share the wishlist between your website and the app: your customers find the same wishlist everywhere, as soon as they are signed in.

Create a Flits API key

The integration uses a Flits API key that you can create yourself, directly from the Flits app.

  • In your Shopify admin, open the "Flits" app
  • Go to "Settings" > "API Keys"
  • Click on "Create API Key", name the key "Makappi", then click on "Generate"
  • Copy the generated key: it is only shown once, right after it is created

Connect Flits to your Mobile App

  • In your Makappi dashboard, go to "Settings" > "Integrations" and select "Flits Wishlist"
  • Enable the integration and paste the key in the "API token" field
  • Click on "Test the connection" to make sure the key works
  • Save and publish the change to finish the integration

What your customers get in the app

  • Customers signed in to their store account see their Flits wishlist in the app
  • Products added to the wishlist in the app also appear on your website, and vice versa

Troubleshooting

  • The token is refused: make sure you copied the whole key, without spaces before or after. If needed, create a new key in the Flits app and try again.
  • The wishlist doesn't show in the app: the customer must be signed in to their store account in the app.
  • Anything else: contact us at support@makappi.com.

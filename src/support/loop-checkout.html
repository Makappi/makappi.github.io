Set up Loop Checkout+

  1. FAQ
  2. Integrations
  3. Set up Loop Checkout+

Let customers choose Checkout+ in their app cart to pay for return coverage.

1. Confirm your Loop setup

Ask your Loop contact to enable Returns Coverage Enabled and confirm that Loop has created your Checkout+ product. Use that product; do not create a replacement. Checkout+ New Extension is not required for this integration.

2. Publish the product in Shopify

Open the Checkout+ product in Shopify > Products. Set its status to Active, add Makappi under publishing / sales channels, and save. Check its availability in the markets where your app sells.

3. Enable the Makappi integration

In Makappi > Settings > Integrations, enable Loop Checkout+, save and publish your configuration. The app retrieves the product and price automatically. No product ID or API key is needed.

4. Check the shopping experience

Test the cart with and without Checkout+. If you use excluded product tags in Loop, also test an excluded cart. If the offer is missing, check the steps above or contact Loop.

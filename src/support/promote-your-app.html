How to promote your App after its launch?

  1. FAQ
  2. Promote your App

Your App is live on the App Store and Google Play: congratulations! Customers will not find it on their own, though. The apps that grow fastest are the ones their brand talks about everywhere, all the time. This page lists the tools Makappi gives you and the actions that work best.

The golden rule: put links to your App everywhere. Every place where a customer sees your brand (website, emails, social networks, packaging, shop) should give them a chance to download it. The more opportunities they have, the more downloads you get.

Why it is worth the effort

Every customer who downloads your App is a customer you can reach directly with push notifications. A push appears on their lock screen, it is free, and you do not depend on an ad budget, a social network algorithm or a crowded inbox. Announce a new collection, a restock or a sale, and your customers know right away.

The more customers have your App, the more powerful this channel becomes. Getting them to download it is the first step: section 6 explains how to make the most of it.

1. Turn on the App Banner on your mobile store

This is the most important tool. Customers who browse your store on their phone are your future App users: they already shop on mobile and already know your brand. The App Banner shows them a small banner with your App icon and a download button, on every page of your store.

  • It only appears on iPhone, iPad and Android, never on desktop, and each device opens the right store.
  • It is never shown at checkout, so it does not get in the way of an order.
  • You choose the message, the button text, the colors and the position (top of the page or fixed at the bottom).
  • In the advanced settings, you can set a display delay, how long it stays hidden after a customer closes it, and the pages where it should not appear.

To set it up, in your Makappi dashboard, go to Settings > App Banner, then follow the three steps:

  1. Customize the banner and save.
  2. Turn on App banner in your Shopify theme editor, then save.
  3. Check the result on your phone: scan the QR code shown in the dashboard.

Tip: use the short message to give a reason to download, for example "€10 off your first order in the App" (see section 5).

2. Add links to your App everywhere

In your Makappi dashboard, go to Settings > App Promotion. You will find:

  • your App Store and Google Play links, ready to copy;
  • the official App Store and Google Play badges, with an HTML code ready to paste into your website or your emails.

Then add them wherever your customers see your brand:

  • Website: the footer of every page, the announcement bar at the top of the site, a dedicated "Our App" page, the order confirmation page, and your newsletter sign-up pop-up, which can promote your App offer.
  • Emails: the footer of your newsletters, the welcome email, order confirmation and shipping emails, and your team's email signatures.
  • Social networks: the link in your Instagram and TikTok bio, a pinned post, Story highlights, your Facebook page.
  • Packaging: a card or a flyer in each parcel, with a QR code to download the App and a clear benefit, such as "Scan to get €10 off your first order in the App".
  • Physical shops and events: a QR code poster at the counter, on receipts, at markets and pop-up stores.

Tip: create an "Our App" page on your Shopify store with both badges, a few screenshots and your offer. You then have one single link that works for every customer, iPhone or Android, which you can turn into a QR code for your printed materials.

3. Set up universal links

With universal links, a link to your store (in an email, an SMS, a social post) opens directly in your App for customers who have installed it, and on your website for the others. Your usual communications then automatically send App users to the App, where they order more.

Follow our guide: How can I set up universal links for iOS and Android?

4. Announce the launch

The launch of your App is news: make the most of it.

  • A dedicated email to your whole customer list, with the store badges, a few screenshots and your offer.
  • An SMS if you have an SMS list: it is read on the phone, right where the App is downloaded.
  • Social posts and Stories: show the App in a short video (a Reel or a TikTok where you browse it and place an order), and pin the post.
  • Your community: ask your ambassadors and partner influencers to talk about it.
  • Ads: if you already run Meta or TikTok ads, target your website visitors and your customers with an ad for the App.

One announcement is not enough. Remind customers about the App regularly in your next newsletters and posts, and add it to your automated emails (welcome series, post-purchase emails) so that every new customer hears about it.

5. Give customers a reason to download it

An offer reserved for the App is what converts best. A simple message works very well: "€10 off your first order in the App", used in your banner, your emails and your posts.

Follow our guide to create a discount code that is almost exclusive to the App, displayed on the App Home page and applied automatically: How to offer discount codes in your App?

Other ideas that work well:

  • early access to new collections and sales for App users;
  • products or bundles available only in the App;
  • free shipping on orders placed in the App, for a limited time;
  • a gift with the first orders placed in the App.

6. Keep your App users engaged

Once the App is installed, push notifications are your most direct channel: they appear on the customer's lock screen. In your Makappi dashboard, go to Marketing > Push Campaigns to send a campaign right away or schedule it.

  • Send one when you have something to say: a new collection, a restock, a sale, an App-only offer.
  • Keep the message short and link it to the right product or collection.
  • Do not send too many: a customer who finds them annoying turns them off.

7. Measure the results

Track who uses the App and who orders from it, then compare before and after each action. Our guide explains how to create these customer segments in Shopify: Identify app customers: customer metafield and segments.

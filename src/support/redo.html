Set up Redo

  1. FAQ
  2. Integrations
  3. Set up Redo

Offer customers a choice of checkout with or without Redo return coverage in your app cart.

1. Complete your Redo setup

Install Redo on your Shopify store and complete its setup with your Redo contact. Confirm that return coverage is enabled and identify the Shopify products used for your offer.

2. Add the Makappi sales channel in Redo

In the Redo dashboard, open Settings > Coverage, add Makappi to the sales channels for your coverage products, and save. Manage this setting from Redo, which generates the products.

3. Check publication in Shopify

In Shopify > Products, open each Redo coverage product. Check its Active status, confirm that Makappi is listed under publishing / sales channels. Check availability in the markets where your app sells. Redo may offer different products depending on the cart.

4. Enable the Makappi integration

In Makappi > Settings > Integrations, enable Redo. Save and publish your configuration. The app automatically retrieves the Team ID from your Shopify domain, then the offer and price. No ID, API key or product selection is required.

5. Check the shopping experience

Test checkout with and without coverage. If the offer is missing, check product availability and coverage activation with Redo. If the issue persists, contact Makappi support to check automatic discovery.
